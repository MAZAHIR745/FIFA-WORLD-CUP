Welcome

Football brings the world together

The FIFA World Cup is an international football competition for men's national teams organised by FIFA. It is one of the world's most watched sporting events, bringing together supporters, cultures and teams from many countries.

World Football Hub is an independent educational fan website. It shares general information about the tournament, its history and the rules of football. It is not an official FIFA website and is not affiliated with FIFA.

Since 1930

A tournament with a remarkable history

The first FIFA World Cup took place in Uruguay in 1930. The hosts defeated Argentina in the final. The competition grew over time and became a global sporting event. The tournament was not held in 1942 or 1946 because of the Second World War, then returned in 1950.

Across generations, the World Cup has produced memorable finals, surprising results and performances that became part of football history.

Legends

Players who shaped the story

Pelé

Pelé won three men's World Cups with Brazil: 1958, 1962 and 1970.

Diego Maradona

Maradona captained Argentina to the 1986 World Cup title.

Lionel Messi

Messi captained Argentina to victory at the 2022 World Cup.

Cristiano Ronaldo

Ronaldo has represented Portugal at multiple World Cups and is among the sport's best-known forwards.

Expanded edition

FIFA World Cup 2026

The 2026 men's FIFA World Cup is hosted by Canada, Mexico and the United States. It is the first edition with 48 teams and 104 matches, expanding the tournament from the 32-team format used from 1998 through 2022.

The basics

Football rules in brief

  • Each team has up to 11 players on the field, including a goalkeeper.
  • A standard senior match lasts 90 minutes: two 45-minute halves, plus added time.
  • The offside rule applies to attacking players in certain positions when a teammate plays the ball.
  • Yellow cards are cautions; a red card means dismissal.
  • In some knockout matches, extra time and a penalty shoot-out are used to decide the winner.
